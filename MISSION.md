# Shepherd Study Mission

This document defines the permanent mission boundaries for Shepherd Study.

## Purpose

Shepherd Study exists to help people return to Scripture, prayer, obedience, and faithful life in the local church.  
It must never present itself as a replacement for Scripture, pastors, or Christian community.

## Guiding Principles

1. Scripture first  
The Bible text is primary. All features must point users back to the text itself.

2. Prayerful dependence  
The app should encourage prayer and reliance on God, not confidence in technology.

3. Obedience over information  
The goal is faithful living, repentance, love, and discipleship, not religious content consumption.

4. Church-rooted discipleship  
The product should strengthen family and local church life, not isolate users into private spirituality.

5. Humility and clarity  
AI is an assistant, not an authority. The app must clearly separate biblical text from AI-generated suggestions.

## Non-Negotiables

1. No replacement of Scripture  
Do not hide, replace, or deprioritize biblical text with summaries.

2. No manipulative engagement design  
No addictive loops, vanity streaks, or retention tricks that reward endless use without obedience.

3. No false authority from AI  
No feature may imply AI is infallible, inspired, or doctrinally final.

4. No exploitation of private spiritual data  
Prayer notes, confessions, and journaling data must never be sold or used for manipulative targeting.

5. No undermining of church/pastoral care  
Features must not position the app as a substitute for pastors, elders, or real Christian fellowship.

## Product Standard

Every new feature must pass this test:

1. Does it lead users back to Scripture and prayer?
2. Does it support concrete obedience and discipleship?
3. Does it strengthen family and local church life?
4. Is AI clearly assistive and transparent?
5. Would we be at peace praying over this feature before release?

If a feature fails any test above, it does not ship.
